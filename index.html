Your guide to Qubit Quest

Welcome to Qubit Quest!

Qubit Quest is an interactive mobile application designed to introduce you to the exciting world of quantum computing. Whether you're a curious beginner or a student looking to solidify your understanding, Qubit Quest provides a hands-on platform to build and simulate quantum circuits.

Our goal is to make learning quantum mechanics accessible and engaging, allowing you to experiment with quantum gates and observe the unique behaviors of qubits.

Key Features

  • Interactive Circuit Builder: Drag and drop quantum gates to construct your own circuits.
  • Real-time Simulation: See how your qubits evolve and measure their states instantly.
  • Gate Library: Access a comprehensive library of quantum gates with detailed explanations (see Gate Documentation).
  • Visual Feedback: Understand complex quantum phenomena like superposition and entanglement through clear visualizations.
  • Guided Quests: Follow structured lessons to build foundational knowledge in quantum computing.

How to Use the App

Navigating Qubit Quest is intuitive. Here's a quick overview:

  • Main Screen: Start new circuits or load previous projects.
  • Circuit Editor: Drag gates from the palette onto your qubit lines.
  • Gate Information: Tap on any gate in the palette for a quick overview, or visit the dedicated Gate Documentation page for in-depth details.
  • Run Simulation: Execute your circuit and view the measurement probabilities or state vector.
  • Reset: Clear your circuit to start fresh.
