Qubit Quest v2.6 workspace mockups

Static desktop layout proposals for milestone M1. They use the app's real colour tokens, gate colours and gate inventory. Each frame is a fixed 1280 px (or 1024 px) canvas; scroll sideways inside a frame on small screens.

1. Composer, nothing selected

Proposed layout

Bell-pair circuit on 3 qubits. The inspector shows a one-line empty state and circuit facts. Results open on the State tab with exact probabilities beside each amplitude.

2. Hadamard selected

Proposed inspector

The inspector replaces the selection toolbar and the routine use of the modal dialog: identity, intuition, matrix and basis mapping are inline. "More…" still opens the existing GateInspectorDialog for the full lesson.

3. Parameterised Rz selected

Proposed angle editor

θ is edited in units of π with presets and a slider; the matrix updates live and the change commits once (one autosave). Replaces both the lesson slider and the post-placement angle dialog.

4. Measurement results

Run once / shots existExact overlay proposed

Bell pair measured into c0 and c1, 1,000 shots. Filled bars are observed frequencies; the amber tick is the exact Born-rule probability from the state before measurement. The single outcome sits above the histogram.

5. Larger five-qubit circuit

Proposed collapse

Palette collapsed to a rail and the inspector to its header, giving the canvas most of the window. Results panel widened by dragging. Zoom controls are shown greyed because zoom is a stretch item pending decision D5.

6. Learning Journey, active chapter selected

Proposed

Ordered chapter list with one state glyph per row, and a detail panel with objectives, lessons and one primary action. Concept links become a "Builds on" line. Progression rules are unchanged.

7. Tablet landscape (1024 px)

Proposed medium tier

Sidebar becomes a rail; palette is an icon rail; Circuit and Results switch with a segmented control instead of squeezing three columns. Same functional components as the wide layout.
