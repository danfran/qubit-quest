--- LEVEL 1 (Bit Flip) — Minor / Misleading ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, line 32
Text: "every quantum gate is its own inverse (or has one)"
Issue: Misleading phrasing. Many gates (T, S, Rx with angle ≠ π) are NOT self-inverse.
       The correct statement is "every quantum gate is invertible (unitary), though not
       every gate is its own inverse." The current wording implies most gates are
       self-inverse, which contradicts the T and S gates introduced later.

--- LEVEL 4 (The i-State) — ERROR 1 ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, line 102
Text: "producing the so-called |i⟩ state (also written |R⟩)"
Issue: WRONG label. The |R⟩ (right-circular) state is conventionally defined as the
       equatorial superposition (|0⟩ + i|1⟩)/√2. This is the state produced in Level 8
       (S Gate). The state Y|0⟩ = i|1⟩ is merely |1⟩ with a global phase of i, which is
       physically indistinguishable from |1⟩ — it is NOT |R⟩.
Suggestion: Remove "(also written |R⟩)". The level's own notation "|i⟩" is fine as an
       internal game label, but equating it to |R⟩ is factually wrong.

--- LEVEL 4 (The i-State) — ERROR 2 ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, line 103
Text: "This is a 90° rotation around the Y-axis of the Bloch sphere."
Issue: WRONG angle. The Pauli Y gate equals i·Ry(π), which is a 180° (π) rotation around
       the Y-axis of the Bloch sphere (up to global phase). A 90° Y-axis rotation is
       Ry(π/2), which produces (|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2 from |0⟩ — a completely different state.
Suggestion: Replace "90°" with "180°":
       "This is a 180° rotation around the Y-axis of the Bloch sphere (Y = i·Ry(π))."

--- LEVEL 18 (Toffoli Gate CCX) — ERROR ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, line 476
Text: "The Toffoli gate is the simplest gate that cannot be simulated efficiently classically."
Issue: Factually WRONG. The Toffoli gate is a deterministic conditional NOT — a classical
       computer simulates it in O(1). Classical simulation of the Toffoli gate is trivial.
       The conceptIntro for the same level already states the correct fact: "Toffoli is
       universal for classical reversible computation."
Suggestion: Replace the sentence with the correct claim, e.g.:
       "Toffoli (CCX) combined with the Hadamard gate forms a universal gate set for
       quantum computation, and on its own it is universal for classical reversible logic."

--- LEVEL 29 (Conditional Phase Flip) — ERROR ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, line 852
Text: "Full circuit: H(Q0), H(Q1), CNOT, H(Q1) — or equivalently H(Q0), CNOT, H(Q1) from |00⟩."
Issue: The two circuits claimed to be equivalent are NOT equivalent, and the "Full circuit"
       is wrong. Tracing H(Q0), H(Q1), CNOT, H(Q1) from |00⟩:
         H(Q0) → (|00⟩+|10⟩)/√2
         H(Q1) → (|00⟩+|01⟩+|10⟩+|11⟩)/2  [uniform superposition]
         CNOT   → still uniform (CNOT is a permutation; the uniform state is invariant)
         H(Q1)  → (|00⟩+|10⟩)/√2           [H·H=I collapses Q1 back to |0⟩]
       This gives (|00⟩+|10⟩)/√2, NOT the target (|00⟩+|01⟩+|10⟩−|11⟩)/2.
       The correct relationship is:
         CZ = H(Q1)·CNOT·H(Q1), applied after uniform superposition H(Q0)·H(Q1):
           H(Q0), H(Q1), H(Q1), CNOT, H(Q1)
         = H(Q0), I, CNOT, H(Q1)           [since H(Q1)·H(Q1) = I]
         = H(Q0), CNOT, H(Q1)              ← the correct 3-gate circuit
Suggestion: Replace the "Full circuit" line with:
       "The explicit decomposition CZ = H(Q1)·CNOT·H(Q1), applied after the uniform
       superposition H(Q0)·H(Q1), gives a 5-gate sequence that simplifies (H·H=I)
       to: H(Q0), CNOT, H(Q1) from |00⟩."

--- LEVEL 37 (GHZ State with Phase) — ERROR ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, lines 1152–1153
Text: "This 'GHZ with a phase' state is one of the 8 maximally-entangled 3-qubit states
       used as logical qubits in the [[3,1,1]] quantum error-correcting code."
Issue: Factually WRONG on two counts.
       1. The [[3,1,1]] code (3 physical qubits, 1 logical qubit, distance 1) uses
          |000⟩ as |0⟩_L and |111⟩ as |1⟩_L — plain computational basis states, NOT
          GHZ-class superpositions. The state (|000⟩−|111⟩)/√2 is simply the logical
          |−⟩_L superposition of those two codewords; it is not itself called "a logical
          qubit" in that code.
       2. The [[3,1,1]] code has distance d=1, meaning it cannot correct any errors
          (it can only detect 1 error). Calling it a useful error-correcting code is
          misleading.
       The 8 GHZ-class states are not defined or used as "logical qubits" in the
       [[3,1,1]] repetition code — they appear in quantum secret sharing, multipartite
       entanglement protocols, and tests of quantum nonlocality.
Suggestion: Replace the last sentence with:
       "GHZ-class states form a complete, maximally-entangled basis for 3-qubit Hilbert
       space and appear in quantum secret sharing and quantum communication protocols."

--- LEVEL 46 (Precise Bloch Sphere Navigation) — ERROR 1 ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, line 1490
Text: "Both amplitudes end up equal (e^(−iπ/8)/√2)."
Issue: WRONG. Applying Ry(π/4) then Rx(π/2) to |0⟩ gives TWO DIFFERENT amplitudes:
       Component 0: (cos(π/8) − i·sin(π/8))/√2 = e^(−iπ/8)/√2 ≈ 0.653 − 0.271i
       Component 1: (sin(π/8) − i·cos(π/8))/√2 = e^(−i3π/8)/√2 ≈ 0.271 − 0.653i
       These have equal magnitude (both 1/√2) but different phases (−π/8 vs −3π/8).
       They are NOT both equal to e^(−iπ/8)/√2. The same error appears in the hint
       at line 1483: "the result is (cos(π/8)−i·sin(π/8))/√2 on both components."
       The solutionFinalVector is also wrong as a consequence: Component 1 is set to
       Complex(sqrt1_2*cos(π/8), −sqrt1_2*sin(π/8)) but should be
       Complex(sqrt1_2*sin(π/8), −sqrt1_2*cos(π/8)).
Suggestion: Correct the solutionExplanation to:
       "Component 0: (cos(π/8)−i·sin(π/8))/√2 = e^(−iπ/8)/√2.
        Component 1: (sin(π/8)−i·cos(π/8))/√2 = e^(−i3π/8)/√2.
        Both have equal magnitude 1/√2 but differ in phase by π/4."
       Also fix solutionFinalVector Component 1 to
       Complex(sqrt1_2 * math.sin(math.pi / 8), -sqrt1_2 * math.cos(math.pi / 8)).

--- LEVEL 46 (Precise Bloch Sphere Navigation) — ERROR 2 ---
File: lib/models/qq_levels.dart, lines 1491–1492
Text: "This two-gate decomposition Ry·Rx is the final form of the Euler rotation
       U = Rz·Ry·Rz — here Rz(0) is trivial."
Issue: WRONG. Setting Rz(0) = I in the Euler decomposition Rz(α)·Ry(β)·Rz(γ) gives
       I·Ry·I = Ry alone — it does NOT reduce to Ry·Rx. Rx and Rz are rotations
       around different axes; one cannot become the other by setting an angle to zero.
       Ry·Rx is a valid two-axis decomposition but it is NOT a degenerate case of
       the ZYZ Euler decomposition.
Suggestion: Replace with a correct statement, e.g.:
       "Two rotations around different axes can reach any point on the Bloch sphere.
        Any single-qubit gate has a ZYZ Euler decomposition Rz(α)·Ry(β)·Rz(γ);
        Ry·Rx here is an alternative two-axis factorisation for this specific target."
